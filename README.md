# 3D Spherical Energy Shield

![Shield Example]([Shield-/YouCut_20260426_150837087.gif](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Cammakingminds/Shield-/refs/heads/main/YouCut_20260426_150837087.gif))

An interactive, procedural 3D energy shield simulation. The lattice dynamically reacts to touch, taking localized structural damage, physically breaching, and mathematically healing itself over time.

## Features

* **Procedural Physics:** Real-time damage propagation, structural stress calculations, and topological healing.
* **Dynamic Audio Synthesis:** A 100% procedural Web Audio engine. Generates electrical crackling, sparks, and distortion mathematically tied to the size of physical breaches and global shield stress.
* **Reactive Visuals:** Dynamic color shifting, edge glitching, and bloom effects based on local heat and structural tension. 
* **Zero Assets:** No external image textures or audio files are used. Everything is generated via math and code.

## Controls

* **Click / Touch & Drag:** Rotate the shield.
* **Press & Hold:** Deal continuous, localized damage to the lattice. Hold until the shield breaches to create a hole.

* JavaScript (ES6+)
